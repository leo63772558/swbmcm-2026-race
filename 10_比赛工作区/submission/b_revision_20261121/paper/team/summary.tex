Buildings obstruct camera lines of sight to hovering low-altitude unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). Five mobile observers can reposition to obtain unobstructed bearings, provided each vehicle clears the buildings and both cameras are ready when a paired observation begins. We use one geometry model throughout, then test how station placement, travel and gimbal timing affect accuracy, response time, and recovery from disruptions.
