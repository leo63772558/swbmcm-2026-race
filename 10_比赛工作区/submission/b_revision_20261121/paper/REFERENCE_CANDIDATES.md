# Candidate references for the B review

Checked on 2026-11-21 against publisher records, an issuing body or original author copies. Some publishers expose metadata only without a subscription. This is a reading list for the team. Move a source into the manuscript only after reading the relevant passage and tying it to an actual citation in the team's text. Number citations in order of first use. No source in this list establishes a result for Problem B.

## Geometry and route construction

1. T. Lozano-Pérez. “Spatial Planning: A Configuration Space Approach.” *IEEE Transactions on Computers*, C-32(2), 108–120, 1983. Review the configuration obstacle construction before writing the clearance model. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TC.1983.1676196) · [Original article](https://people.csail.mit.edu/jbarry/spring2011PR2/readings/cspaceorig.pdf)
2. J. R. Shewchuk. “Adaptive Precision Floating-Point Arithmetic and Fast Robust Geometric Predicates.” *Discrete & Computational Geometry*, 18, 305–363, 1997. Relevant when discussing predicate robustness near tangencies. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/PL00009321) · [Author's article index](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~quake/robust.html)
3. E. W. Dijkstra. “A Note on Two Problems in Connexion with Graphs.” *Numerische Mathematik*, 1, 269–271, 1959. Background for the shortest path subproblem on a fixed graph. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01386390)
4. P. E. Hart, N. J. Nilsson, and B. Raphael. “A Formal Basis for the Heuristic Determination of Minimum Cost Paths.” *IEEE Transactions on Systems Science and Cybernetics*, 4(2), 100–107, 1968. Read only if comparing graph search alternatives. The article's graph optimization result should not be carried over to a finite station screen or beam search. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSSC.1968.300136) · [Original article](https://ai.stanford.edu/~nilsson/OnlinePubs-Nils/PublishedPapers/astar.pdf)

## Bearing intersection and uncertainty

5. A. N. Bishop, B. Fidan, B. D. O. Anderson, K. Doğançay, and P. N. Pathirana. “Optimality Analysis of Sensor-Target Localization Geometries.” *Automatica*, 46(3), 479–492, 2010. Relevant to how station geometry changes localization information. Match the paper's measurement model to the team's before comparing optima. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.automatica.2009.12.003) · [Publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0005109809005500)
6. Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology. *Evaluation of Measurement Data: Supplement 1 to the Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement. Propagation of Distributions Using a Monte Carlo Method*. JCGM 101:2008. Relevant to uncertainty propagation and Monte Carlo protocol. [Official publication and DOI record](https://www.bipm.org/en/doi/10.59161/jcgm101-2008)
7. E. B. Wilson. “Probable Inference, the Law of Succession, and Statistical Inference.” *Journal of the American Statistical Association*, 22(158), 209–212, 1927. Primary source for the score interval used to construct the recorded simulation intervals. Check the separate multiplicity correction in the implementation. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1927.10502953) · [Publisher record](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1927.10502953)
8. L. D. Brown, T. T. Cai, and A. DasGupta. “Interval Estimation for a Binomial Proportion.” *Statistical Science*, 16(2), 101–133, 2001. Original binomial interval comparison for the audit of finite sampling precision. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/ss/1009213286) · [Article copy](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~brian/720/week01/Binomial-StatSci.pdf)

## Dispatch and recourse

9. H. N. Psaraftis. “A Dynamic Programming Solution to the Single Vehicle Many-to-Many Immediate Request Dial-a-Ride Problem.” *Transportation Science*, 14(2), 130–154, 1980. Historical example of sequencing coupled vehicle service requests. Its constraints differ from synchronized dual observation. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/trsc.14.2.130) · [Publisher record](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/trsc.14.2.130)
10. M. W. P. Savelsbergh and M. Sol. “The General Pickup and Delivery Problem.” *Transportation Science*, 29(1), 17–29, 1995. Candidate source for vehicle routing and service constraints as related work. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/trsc.29.1.17) · [Publisher record](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/trsc.29.1.17)
11. A. Stentz. “Optimal and Efficient Path Planning for Partially-Known Environments.” *Proceedings of the 1994 IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation*, 3310–3317, 1994. Related work for replanning after information changes. The archived event-based dispatcher does not implement this algorithm. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ROBOT.1994.351061) · [Original research record](https://publications.ri.cmu.edu/optimal-and-efficient-path-planning-for-partially-known-environments)
12. Y. Van Tendeloo and H. Vangheluwe. “Discrete Event System Specification Modeling and Simulation.” *2018 Winter Simulation Conference*, 162–176, 2018. Candidate source for describing event times and state transitions. Use only for a specific comparison with the team's replay semantics. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/WSC.2018.8632372) · [Publisher record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8632372/)
